# Educational-use disclaimer

## Article version

**For education and demonstration only.** This project shows how DHIS2 can be connected to
Power BI and to Shiny for Python to support public health data analysis, policy planning and
resource allocation. It is intended for educational, demonstration and technical learning purposes.

- The data come from the public DHIS2 demo playground (Sierra Leone demo database). The data and
  every output derived from them must not be read as verified evidence of the current performance
  of any real health facility, chiefdom or district.
- The predictive model demonstrates one possible analytical approach. It is not a validated
  clinical or operational decision-making system, and its risk estimates are not judgements on
  any facility or health worker.
- Its estimates depend on the quality and completeness of the data, the suitability of the
  selected variables, the model's assumptions and the way it is validated. In this project it was
  checked on a single quarter of demo data only.
- Any real-world use would require further validation on real data over several periods,
  appropriate data governance and protection, and interpretation by qualified public health
  stakeholders who know the local context.
